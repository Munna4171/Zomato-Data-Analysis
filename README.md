# Zomato Restaurant Market Analysis 🍽️

## 📌 Project Objective
To analyze restaurant data from Zomato to uncover actionable business insights regarding customer engagement, pricing strategies, and service offerings. The project involved end-to-end data processing, from raw data cleaning to interactive visual storytelling.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Data Cleaning & Exploratory Analysis:** Python (Pandas)
* **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Tableau

## 💡 Key Business Discoveries
1. **The Digital Multiplier:** Restaurants offering online delivery see a **7.4x increase** in customer engagement (averaging 559 votes vs. 75 votes) and maintain a notably higher overall rating.
2. **The Premium Proxy:** Customers are highly willing to pay a premium for guaranteed seating. Restaurants with table booking cost 72% more on average but boast a stellar average rating of 4.19/5.
3. **The Value Paradox:** Lower prices do not equate to higher satisfaction. Standard "Dining" restaurants are the cheapest category but have the lowest average ratings, while more expensive "Buffet" options score significantly higher.
4. **The Popularity Bias:** A strong positive correlation exists between foot traffic (total votes) and overall restaurant rating, indicating that high-turnover establishments consistently maintain higher quality scores.
